Two-scale — abc() + shellPeak through twoScale

A recipe rather than a knob: an organized large-scale backbone carrying incoherent broadband detail. The backbone here is abc() — the classical ABC cell flow, written as exactly three waves of the same engine, with no randomness in it at all. The detail is an ordinary field with a shellPeak spectrum, a narrow band around kDetail. twoScale sums them; the sum of two divergence-free fields is divergence-free and its potential is the sum of theirs, so obstacles and the potential bakes keep working on the result.

The point of the kDetail slider: sizing the detail as amplitude = C_TWO_SCALE / kDetail holds its vorticity budget fixed. Slide it and the detail moves to finer scales without the picture getting busier or calmer — watch the enstrophy readout stay put while the texture changes.
