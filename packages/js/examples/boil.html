The frame-0 identity

Over 4096 points, max |uflutter(x, 0) − u0(x, 0)| = —. Not "small" — exactly zero, and structurally so: flutter is built from phases that were already drawn (it consumes no RNG), the phase term is written as sin(ω_f t + ph) − sin(ph) so it cancels at t = 0, and the RMS normalization is measured there too. Hit jump to t = 0 and the two panels become pixel-identical.

Flutter rides the churn clock

They are not independent dials. The flutter rate is φ·rate₀·kmax^⅔ and rate₀ ∝ churn, so with churn at 0 the difference between flutter 2 and flutter 0 is — at t = 3.7. Drag churn to zero and both dials die together — a frozen field stays frozen no matter how hard you shake this one.
